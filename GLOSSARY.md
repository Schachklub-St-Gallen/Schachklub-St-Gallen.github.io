# Schachklub St. Gallen

The club website: news, tournaments and the club's teams in the Swiss team championships run by the Schweizerischer Schachbund (swisschess).

## Team championships

**SMM**:
Schweizerische Mannschaftsmeisterschaft, the summer team championship. Played on Sundays from about March to October.
_Avoid_: Mannschaftsmeisterschaft (ambiguous with **SGM**)

**SGM**:
Schweizerische Gruppenmeisterschaft, the winter team championship. Played on Saturdays from about November to March.
_Avoid_: Gruppenmeisterschaft

**Saison**:
One edition of the **SMM** or **SGM**, named by the calendar year it ends in. SGM 2026 is played from November 2025 to March 2026.
_Avoid_: naming an SGM season by the year it starts in, "2025/26"

**Mannschaft**:
One of the club's teams in a **Saison**, named "St. Gallen 1", "St. Gallen 2" and so on.
_Avoid_: Team (in page text)

**Liga**:
A division level within the **SMM** or **SGM**. SMM levels: NLA, NLB, 1. Liga to 4. Liga. SGM levels: 1. and 2. Bundesliga, 1. to 3. Regionalliga.

**Gruppe**:
A single round-robin group within a **Liga**. The **SGM** calls it a "Zone" in page text, for example "2. Bundesliga, Zone B".

**Runde**:
One match day of a **Gruppe**. Every **Mannschaft** in the group plays one match against another.
